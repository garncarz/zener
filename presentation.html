Computational rheology for rock

Reading rock in time

How a nonlinear Zener model turns stress history into strain, and how the repository searches for the hidden parameters.

0:00-1:00. Open with the practical question: when a rock is loaded, how much deformation is immediate, delayed, or history-dependent? This project answers it with a forward model and an inverse search.
The question

One stress curve. Several clocks.

Two samples can see the same stress and still accumulate different strain because their internal relaxation times differ.

Goal: explain the observed strain as the sum of mechanisms that live on different time scales.

σ(t) → ε(t)

Stress history is the input. Total strain is the observable.

1:00-2:00. Establish that this is not just curve fitting. The time structure is the scientific content: fast response, slow response, and nonlinearity all leave different signatures.

The nonlinear core

Instantaneous strain is not quite Hooke's law

εNL(σ) = 1 / EmNL · [ σ + Alpha · sign(σ) · |σ|Beta ]

fEpsNL keeps the sign of stress while allowing a power-law departure from linearity.

Small β and large β do different things to the curve; Alpha controls how visible the nonlinear term becomes.

3:15-4:30. Point at sign and absolute value: the nonlinear correction is symmetric in magnitude while preserving tension/compression direction. EmNL is a scale, Alpha and Beta shape the departure.
Time dependence

Relaxation is memory, implemented as history

Each stress segment has a rate Rs and duration Tp. The model carries the endpoint stress into the next segment.

The exponential terms in fEps encode how earlier loading decays into the present.

What can go wrong?

A good fit is not automatically a true explanation

  • Identifiability: different chains can produce nearly the same strain curve.
  • Scale: moduli and times span orders of magnitude.
  • Numerics: long histories can overflow or create NaNs.
  • Objective: fitting total strain can hide a bad component decomposition.

Questions to ask

Does the fit generalize to a new loading history?

Are parameters physically plausible?

What does each component contribute?

13:00-14:15. This is where the model becomes a scientific instrument rather than a leaderboard. Encourage validation on held-out signal histories and inspection of component plots.
